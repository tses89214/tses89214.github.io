## The Evolution of Our Data Team

A Medium series (Chinese and English) about how a data team with little documentation and few processes slowly became one with documentation, processes and infrastructure it can trust. It is less about new technology than about what changed in the way we work.

The order follows cause and effect:

- **Culture:** make knowledge something that can be written down, so everyone speaks the same language.
- **Tech:** build a foundation the team can trust.
- **AI:** grow data products on that foundation, so data supports decisions instead of only delivering dashboards.
